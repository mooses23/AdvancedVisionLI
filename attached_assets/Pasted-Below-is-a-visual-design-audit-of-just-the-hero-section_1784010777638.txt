Below is a visual design audit of just the hero section. I’ll go layer by layer as if I were writing specifications for a designer or AI image generator.

⸻

Overall Feel

The hero is designed to communicate:

* Modern medical practice
* Premium but approachable
* Child-focused without looking childish
* Calm, optimistic, trustworthy
* Editorial magazine meets healthcare website

Instead of looking like a corporate hospital, it feels almost like an Apple landing page mixed with a pediatric wellness brand.

Psychological Design Principles Used

* Editorial hierarchy: One dominant message, one highlighted phrase, minimal competing elements.
* Directional cueing: The Brock string guides the eye from the photograph toward the headline and back.
* Soft biomorphic shapes: Rounded circles and blobs reduce the clinical feel associated with healthcare.
* Warm–cool balance: Cool aqua backgrounds establish calm; coral accents create urgency and focus.
* Authenticity over stock imagery: The subject is engaged in a real therapy exercise rather than smiling at the camera, increasing credibility.
* Generous whitespace: Empty space creates a premium perception and improves readability.
* Asymmetrical balance: The text-heavy left and image-heavy right remain visually equal without mirroring one another.
* Layered depth: Gradients, translucent circles, organic shapes, and subtle patterns create richness while remaining unobtrusive.

Overall, the hero succeeds because every element serves a purpose: the typography delivers the message, the girl demonstrates the solution, the Brock string connects the two halves, and the restrained color palette creates a calm, trustworthy first impression.
