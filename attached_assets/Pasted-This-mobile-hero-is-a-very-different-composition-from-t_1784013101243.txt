Why It Works

Several subtle decisions make the hero feel premium rather than like a responsive desktop page:

* The girl’s portrait is enlarged relative to the viewport, making her feel immersive rather than decorative.
* The Brock string acts as a visual guide, pulling the eye from the lower-left CTA area back toward the girl’s face.
* Background elements are oversized and partially cropped, avoiding the “sticker” look common on mobile layouts.
* Only one phrase (“20/20 vision”) receives an accent color, giving the eye a single focal point.
* Buttons span almost the full content width, making them thumb-friendly without overwhelming the layout.
* The typography remains the dominant element, even though the portrait occupies nearly half of the hero.

The result is a hero that feels intentionally designed for mobile instead of a compressed desktop layout.
